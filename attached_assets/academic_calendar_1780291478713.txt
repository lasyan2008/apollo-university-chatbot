=== GENERAL ACADEMIC REGULATIONS ===
=============================================================

ATTENDANCE:
- Minimum 80% attendance is required in the current semester to be eligible to write Semester End Examinations.
- Students with less than 80% attendance will be detained on recommendation of HoD.
- Detained students cannot register for next semester and must repeat the same semester by paying tuition fees.

EVALUATION SYSTEM (CBCS - Choice Based Credit System):
- Medium of instruction: English
- Theory Course: Continuous Evaluation (40 marks) + Semester-End Examination (60 marks)
- Continuous Evaluation: Mid-Term Examinations (20 marks) + Quizzes/Assignments/Presentations (10 marks) + other components
- Mid-Term Examinations: Three exams conducted; average of best two taken (out of 20 marks)
- Minimum passing: 40% aggregate with minimum 24 marks (40%) in Semester-End Examination

GRADING SYSTEM:
- O (Outstanding): 90 and above - 10 Grade Points
- A+ (Excellent): 80-89 - 9 Grade Points
- A (Very Good): 70-79 - 8 Grade Points
- B+ (Good): 60-69 - 7 Grade Points
- B (Above Average): 55-59 - 6 Grade Points
- C (Average): 50-54 - 5 Grade Points
- P (Pass): 40-49 - 4 Grade Points
- F (Fail): Below 40 - 0 Grade Points

SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average):
- Calculated as sum of [Credits x Grade Points] divided by total credits in that semester

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average):
- Calculated using same formula but considers all courses in all semesters

CLASS CLASSIFICATION:
- First Class with Distinction: CGPA > 8.0 (passed all courses in first attempt every semester)
- First Class: CGPA > 6.5
- Second Class: CGPA > 5.5
- Pass Class: CGPA > 5.0

MAXIMUM DURATION:
- Students must complete the program within N+2 years (N = minimum program duration)

REVALUATION:
- Students can apply within 7 days from date of publication of results
- Application through proper channel with revaluation fee
- Script valued by second examiner
- If difference > 15 marks, third examiner values it
- Average of nearest two marks declared as final marks

BETTERMENT OF GRADES:
- Students with Pass or Second Class can appear for Betterment Examinations in Theory courses
- Conducted during Summer Vacation
- Permitted only once, immediately after completion of program
